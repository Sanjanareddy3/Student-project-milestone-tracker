# Curriculum Inventory: Regular B.Tech CSE

## 1. Purpose

This document records the available course information for the regular B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) program at ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education (IFHE), Hyderabad.

The inventory supports a curriculum review focused on readiness for industry needs through 2030.

**Scope:** Regular B.Tech CSE, 2023 admission batch. Specialized CSE programs, including AI/ML or Data Science variants, are outside this review.

## 2. Source and Verification Notes

The initial inventory was prepared using the official IFHE program structure information and course-credit screenshots supplied for this review.

Official program structure page: https://www.ifheindia.org/icfai-tech-school-hyderabad/icfaitech-btech-program-structure

The current webpage may not represent the exact curriculum applicable to the 2023 admission batch. Therefore, course names, credits, semester totals, and degree requirements must be reconciled with the original batch-specific curriculum before this inventory is considered final.

## 4. Inventory Quality Checks

The following checks are required before finalizing the inventory:

1. Confirm that all eight semesters are represented.
2. Verify every course name and credit value against the original 2023 batch curriculum.
3. Identify omitted courses, electives, laboratories, projects, and other required components.
4. Recalculate every semester total from its individual course credits.
5. Investigate discrepancies between recorded course totals and published semester totals.
6. Calculate the overall degree total only after all required credits have been accounted for.

## 5. Curriculum Review Categories

Once the inventory is verified, courses can be classified as follows:

- **KEEP:** Retain essential foundational content.
- **UPDATE:** Modernize topics, tools, practical work, or assessments.
- **REDUCE:** Remove unnecessary duplication only when syllabus evidence supports it.
- **ADD:** Introduce missing skills through suitable modules, electives, or formally approved courses.

These categories are recommendations for review and do not represent official university decisions.

## 6. Limitations

This inventory contains recorded course information but is not yet a complete, batch-verified curriculum audit. Some semester totals and course components remain unresolved. No course or credit should be added, removed, or changed based solely on an assumption.

## 7. Conclusion

The inventory provides a starting point for reviewing the regular B.Tech CSE curriculum against emerging industry requirements. A complete and accurate comparison depends on verifying every course, credit, and semester total against the curriculum applicable to students admitted in 2023.
