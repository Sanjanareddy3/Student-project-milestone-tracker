# Task 2: B.Tech CSE Curriculum Audit — 2030 Readiness

## 1. Objective

Evaluate the B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering curriculum at IFHE Hyderabad for its readiness for technology careers in 2030. Identify courses to keep, update, reduce, or add, and propose evidence-based improvements while maintaining a balanced credit structure.

## 2. Student and Program Details

- **University:** IFHE Hyderabad
- **Program:** B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering
- **Admission Year:** 2023
- **Expected Graduation:** 2027
- **Curriculum Baseline:** Official program-structure screenshots provided for this audit

## 3. Curriculum Source and Verification

The official IFHE Hyderabad B.Tech program-structure page and the provided screenshots will serve as the initial curriculum reference.

**Official source:** https://www.ifheindia.org/icfai-tech-school-hyderabad/icfaitech-btech-program-structure

The exact match between the currently published structure and the curriculum applicable to the 2023 admission batch remains to be verified. Original course names and credits will be preserved when recording the baseline.

## 4. Audit Methodology

Each course will be evaluated using four categories:

- **KEEP:** Retain the course with minimal changes.
- **UPDATE:** Modernize content, tools, practical work, or learning outcomes.
- **REDUCE:** Reduce overlapping or less relevant content where justified.
- **ADD:** Introduce a missing topic or course that supports future career requirements.

The audit will consider technical foundations, AI and data skills, cybersecurity, cloud computing, software engineering, practical experience, and industry requirements.

## 5. Evidence Collection

The audit will use:
1. The provided curriculum screenshots.
2. Relevant technology and workforce reports.
3. At least three dated job descriptions.
4. AI-assisted exercises across five subjects, with outputs and verification notes.

Recommendations will distinguish verified facts from proposed changes and assumptions.

## 6. Proposed Deliverables

- Curriculum baseline and course-credit inventory.
- KEEP / UPDATE / REDUCE / ADD recommendations.
- Evidence-based reasons for curriculum changes.
- Comparison with job descriptions.
- Five documented AI-assisted learning exercises.
- Credit-balanced revised curriculum proposal.
- Connection to the Student Project Milestone Tracker capstone.
- Interactive comparison of original and proposed courses and credits.

## 7. Status

**In progress:** Establishing the curriculum baseline and collecting evidence.
