## 3. Credit Verification Method

The credit audit should follow these steps:

1. Identify the official curriculum applicable to students admitted to regular B.Tech CSE in 2023.
2. Record every course name and its credit value for all eight semesters.
3. Distinguish core courses, electives, laboratories, projects, internships, and thesis or seminar requirements where applicable.
4. Calculate each semester's total by adding its individual course credits.
5. Compare the calculated totals with any published semester totals.
6. Investigate discrepancies before changing the inventory or comparison webpage.
7. Calculate the overall program total only after all semesters and mandatory requirements are accounted for.

## 5. Principles for Credit Allocation

- Preserve the officially approved total credits unless the university approves a formal curriculum revision.
- Avoid adding credit requirements without evaluating workload, prerequisites, and accreditation requirements.
- Reduce duplicated content only after comparing the actual syllabi.
- Prefer practical assignments and updated learning materials within existing courses where feasible.
- Introduce new electives or courses through the university's formal academic approval process.

## 6. Limitations

The available program structure may not exactly match the curriculum applicable to the 2023 admission batch. Course names, credit values, semester totals, electives, and internship or thesis requirements require confirmation against the original batch-specific documentation.

No credit values should be changed solely on the basis of industry trends or proposed curriculum improvements.

## 7. Conclusion

A reliable credit comparison must begin with a complete, batch-specific course inventory. Once all course credits and semester totals have been confirmed, curriculum improvements can be assessed while maintaining the approved credit structure wherever possible.
