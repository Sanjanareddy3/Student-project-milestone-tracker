## 6. Credit Calculation Rules

1. Record each original course and credit from the verified curriculum document.
2. Sum the credits for each semester.
3. Check electives, internship credits, thesis credits, and any zero-credit requirements.
4. Record proposed changes in a separate column.
5. Calculate the difference as proposed credits minus original credits.
6. Do not claim credit neutrality until both totals have been verified.

## 7. Conclusion

The initial recommendation is to strengthen practical, project-based learning within the existing curriculum. A final credit-balanced proposal requires verification of the complete curriculum applicable to the 2023 admission batch.
