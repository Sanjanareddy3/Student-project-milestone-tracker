# Task 2 — Curriculum audit and redesign

**Status: Not yet completed.** This folder is a placeholder so the repository has the required separation for both tasks. Do not submit it as a completed curriculum audit.

## Required work

1. Confirm the exact degree, branch, admission batch and curriculum version applicable to your own programme.
2. Link official syllabus/regulation sources and record access dates.
3. Build a semester-wise course map with course code, title, credits, prerequisites and elective alternatives.
4. Classify existing courses as KEEP, UPDATE or REDUCE; label additions as ADD.
5. Compare at least three real, dated job descriptions relevant to the chosen role.
6. Test AI on one authorized, non-confidential exercise in each of five subjects and retain prompts, outputs and human verification.
7. Propose changes affecting approximately 20–25% of programme credits, calculate the exact share from a verified baseline, and preserve a balanced credit total.
8. Connect the Task 1 workflow idea to a proposed lab/capstone with acceptance tests.
9. Produce a report, evidence appendices and a small interactive view.

## Integrity note

Do not infer the applicable curriculum from a current admissions page alone. Do not fabricate job descriptions, source dates, AI errors or faculty feedback. Mark inaccessible evidence as unavailable.
